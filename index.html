A Bear in Space

Well, this took a bit.

I really wanted to make the background a starfield. I knew this could be done in plain HTML and CSS. And I tend to be a minimalist, and love CSS.

I initially made a bunch of radial-gradients in the background of the body. Background position for each. Then I made a ::before pseudo element on the body. This was for animated stars. This issue that came along was getting individual stars to animate. It kept just looking like a starburst from the middle of the page.

Not the effect I was going for. I tried messing with opacity fading during the animation so stars would fade in and out, but since the whole background follows the same animation cycle, that didn't work.

After a bunch of tweaks and reviewing MDN, I bit the bullet and added some aria-hidden spans. Full admission, I did have AI help come up with a random distribution for placing all the static stars in the background. But the rest is built by hand.

Which leads me to a final point. With this project, I want to hand build it. I miss the joy of coding and debugging with nothing but you, the browser, and code...with a splash of MDN docs.

Thanks for stopping by.
